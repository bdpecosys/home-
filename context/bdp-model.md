# BD Partners — the model

*Last updated: 2026-09-22. Internal shared knowledge. Facts only; uncertain items marked \[VERIFY].*

## What it is

BD Partners (bdpartners.co) is an internaionl business-development practice for fintech and payments companies. It sells senior BD as a service and method, rather than as a hire: origination, partnerships, market entry and deal support, delivered by operators who do the legwork themselves.

## What it sells

1. **Market-entry engagements** — opening a named market for a client against a fixed date: research, target mapping, outreach, meeting programme around an anchor event, and conversion. Priced as staged milestone fees. The Cal Platform Italy pilot is the reference engagement (`cal-engagement.md`).
2. **Fractional BD retainer** — ongoing origination and partnership work for a client with no BD muscle in seat. Eko.in is the current example: a low monthly retainer plus a 10% referral fee. 
3. **Referral products** — third-party offerings BD Partners can introduce, which also serve as a legitimate reason to open a senior conversation:

   * Thunes — 10% referral fee.
   * Bridgerpay — 10% referral fee.
   * Xenaris — advisory equity plus revenue share.
   * MelodiCapital — CFO-as-a-service, offered jointly.
4. **In build: the market-entry engine** — a methodology plus agent-based tooling that makes each engagement faster and repeatable across clients (`engine-method.md`).

## Positioning

The competitor is the big-four strategy deck the client already paid for and was disappointed by: comprehensive, hedged, no clear next step. BD Partners sells the opposite — two operators rather than advisors, a signals-based origination method, and recommendations whose reasoning the client's board can interrogate line by line. Data vendors win on scale of data; BD Partners wins on judgment shown transparently, plus execution.

## Commercial state, September 2026

* One large engagement in closing: Cal Platform Italy pilot, NIS 100K over 3–4 months, expected to start early October 2026.
* One small paying fractional client: Eko.in.
* Referral paths live via Thunes, including a path through Max Credit and Bank Leumi. 
* A second market-entry client is being sought on the same model as Cal.

## What experience has established

* Sales cycles are long and fragile: one prospect ran roughly four months of engagement and died immediately after a positive CEO meeting. Nothing is counted as revenue until it is signed.
* What produced the Cal engagement was a referral product that created a reason to call, plus a genuine first-degree door — not firmographic targeting alone.
* Retainer-only pricing is slow to scale; milestone-based engagement fees with defined outputs have worked better.
