Internal work happens in whichever language is faster. Skills, templates, schemas and market-facing material are written in English (see `house-style.md`).## Cadence and decision rights* Weekly 45-minute pipeline review, plus ad-hoc work sessions.* Decisions inside each person's own area are theirs to make; anything that changes scope, price, client commitments or the shared method needs both.* A decision that is made gets written into the relevant knowledge file the same week, or it did not happen.## Working infrastructure (as of 22 September 2026)* Shared workspace: Claude Team, two seats, purchased September 2026.* micha@bdpartners.co created on the BD Partners Google Workspace; outreach goes out from the company domain, not personal addresses.* Private GitHub repository as the method library.* Hardware constraint: at least one 4GB Windows 11 Home laptop; builds must fit that envelope.## Open items between the two of us* **A written one-page agreement covering ownership of the methodology, how revenue from a second client splits, and what happens if one of us steps back.** Not yet in place; it should be signed before the method repository fills up.
