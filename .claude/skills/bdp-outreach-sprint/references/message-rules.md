# Row score and message rules

## Row score (0–2 each, /8, always show the breakdown)
| Line | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit** | In the ICP, with a new market or new unit in play | Adjacent: domestic-only, a connector or an incumbent used as a route, or weak budget | Outside the ICP |
| **Signal age** | ≤6 months | 6–12 months | >12 months (a real but old signal can stay; no signal means no row) |
| **Door** | WARM | KNOWN, senior | Junior or indirect |
| **Role** | Owns expansion or partnerships (CEO/founder, GM of a region, VP or head of BD/partnerships/strategy, corp dev) | Other C-level (CFO, COO) | Anything else: ask for a pointer |

Rank by score, then WARM before KNOWN, then the most recent evidence. Name an alternate door in notes when role < 2.

## Message rules
- **≤90 words.** Count them, whatever the script.
- **Open with their dated signal:** theirs, specific, verifiable. Congratulate or ask about it; don't summarise it back at length.
- **One line on BD Partners**, taken from `bdp-model.md`. In runs 1–2: we open new markets for fintech and payments companies, anchored to a live event in the target market and paid by milestone.
- **One question about their next market.**
- **One ask:** 20 minutes, or a pointer to the right person. For a role-0 door, the pointer is the main ask.
- **Language:** English by default. Use Hebrew for Israeli contacts on WhatsApp.
- **Never name** referral partners or pre-signature clients without their permission. Make no claims you can't verify, invent no shared history, and write nothing that implies warmth that isn't there.
- **Timing:** avoid sending during a holiday week. Say "after the holidays" when needed.

## Skeletons (fill in; don't send as-is)

**English, LinkedIn:**
> [Name], [specific signal, with month]. [BD Partners line]. [Next-market question]? 20 minutes after [date], or a pointer to whoever owns it?

**Hebrew, WhatsApp:**
> היי [שם], ראיתי [האות, עם חודש]. [שורת BD Partners]. [שאלה על השוק הבא]? אשמח ל-20 דקות אחרי [מועד], או להפניה לאדם הנכון.

**Pointer ask (role 0):**
> [Name], [signal]. [BD Partners line]. Who owns [market/region] at [company]? A pointer is all I need.

**Old signal (>12 months, WARM founder):**
> [Name], it's been [N] months since you announced [market]. How is it going? [BD Partners line]. What's next after [market]?
