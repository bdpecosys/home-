# Internal Memo — Call with [Person] ([Company]), [Date]
### Prepared [date] | Sources: [filing name + date], press, BDP pipeline history

## 1. Where [Company] is right now (60-second read)
Structure, ownership, key officers. The central tension in the numbers (2-4 sentences, concrete figures bolded). The recent strategic decision that shapes this call, with its date.

## 2. Reading [Person]
Role, committees, authority position (is the contact the approval body?). Personal comp incentives. Career background and what it forbids pitching. Open question about their motive for taking the call — flagged, not assumed.

## 3. Why-now signals (theirs, not yours)
3-6 numbered signals, each: evidence → implication for what they need.

## 4. The plays (ranked — and what NOT to pitch)
**Play 1 (the pitch, if discovery confirms):** offer + structure + concrete economics + a zero-risk commitment device ("define the box; 2-3 names in 30 days; fee only on success").
**Play 2 (soft mention, no ask).**
**Play 3 (hold back unless they open the door).**
**Do NOT:** explicit list. Honest qualification: paying client X%; partnership realistic/not; network value.

## 5. Discovery questions (use 4-5, in order)
1. Informed-open question proving homework and letting them define their situation
2-4. Box/process/gap questions
5-6. Commitment device + "who else should I be talking to?"

## 6. Facts to drop for credibility (2-3 max, sparingly)
Short quotable lines with numbers. Plus the personal-continuity thread.

## 7. One hygiene line for later (not for this call)
Conflict/disclosure policy if relevant.
